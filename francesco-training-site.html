Session Formats

Training built around how the player learns best

1-on-1 Training

Focused individual sessions built around a specific player's game — technical weaknesses, positional habits, or confidence on the ball. The fastest way to see targeted improvement.

Small Group Sessions

Two to four players training together, keeping the personal attention of 1-on-1 work while adding the competitive edge and game-realistic reps that come from training alongside others.

Group Training

Larger sessions for teams or friend groups looking to train together — technical drills, small-sided games, and conditioning work suited to the group's level.

What's Covered

Three pillars, every session

Technical Training

Ball mastery, first touch, dribbling, passing, shooting, and game IQ — broken down and rebuilt through repetition until it holds up under pressure.

Physical Training

Speed, agility, and conditioning work scaled to the player's age and current fitness level, building the athleticism the technical work needs to show up in games.

Dietary Recommendations

Simple, age-appropriate nutrition guidance to support training and recovery — practical habits, not restrictive rules.

Mentorship

Faith-based mindset training

Alongside technical and physical training, Francesco incorporates a faith-based mindset approach — building discipline, integrity, and resilience into the way players think and compete, on and off the field.

Sessions are built around technical and physical training either way — this mindset layer is simply part of how Francesco coaches.

About

Trained by someone who's still in it

Francesco Bonavita is a competitive NCAA soccer player at Stevens Institute of Technology and the founder of this training program, based in Miami-Dade. Training players since 2023, Francesco works with individuals and small groups across the U9–U17 range, meeting each player at their current level and building a plan around it — not a generic curriculum.

Every session pulls from the same standards used in high-level competitive play: technical precision, physical readiness, and the daily habits — including nutrition — that support both.

"Every player improves differently. Sessions are built around the player, not the other way around."
